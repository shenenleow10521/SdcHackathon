Everything the Domain 1 brief asked for

Aiversecity asked for six capabilities. Each one is working in the prototype, and each one is somewhere on Aisyah's map.

  1. Skills profile intake

    CV or LinkedIn upload, follow-up questions from the interviewer agent, and a confirm-and-rate checklist.

    first-time setup

  2. AI-trend signal layer

    Dated, sourced signals from the market scout, filtered by rules, approved by a person and stored as versions.

    agents panel · review queue

  3. Future-role matching

    A match score for each role from evidence-weighted skills, with the gap shown in weeks of learning.

    jobs tab · career map

  4. Personalised reskilling paths

    Fastest, No-regret and Safest routes fitted to weekly hours, with courses and the reason each step works.

    path tab

  5. SDG mapping

    Every job and skill tagged with the goals it supports, each with a one-line reason.

    job and skill details

  6. More than one future

    Moderate or fast AI adoption, a 2026 to 2030 timeline, and a No-regret route that holds up in both.

    forecast controls

Agents read. Formulas score. People decide.

AI does the reading and the talking. Every number on screen comes from plain code and a sourced record, so the same input always gives the same answer.

  1. Market scout

    Searches AI research and vendor news for each tracked task, and must copy an exact quote and link.

  2. Fixed rules

    Drop anything with no source, a quote that isn't on the page, or a duplicate.

  3. AI reviewer

    A second model checks the claim against its evidence and reports its confidence.

  4. Human approval

    Big, single-source or route-changing updates wait for a person.

  5. Reroute

    Profiles are re-scored on the new knowledge version, and each worker sees what changed.

How a match is scored

Match =Σ (skill weight × your coverage)Σ skill weight

Skill weight reflects how often employers ask for the skill. Coverage is your level divided by the level the job needs. Agents may only choose skills, jobs and courses that exist in the database, and a fact checker removes any claim without a source.
