When AI changes the road, HalaTuju reroutes your career.

Upload your CV. See which of your skills AI is taking over, which jobs you can reach, and how many weeks of learning it takes. When the job market moves, your route updates, and every change shows its source.

Try it as Aisyah See her first week

HalaTuju means “direction” in Malay. This is a working prototype for SDC Hackathon 2026 (Domain 1). The people, numbers and courses in it are sample data.

Aisyah has 90 days and six hours a week.

She has spent nine years in accounts payable at a shared-services centre in Cyberjaya. Her company has just announced AI will process its invoices. She is not alone: TalentCorp's 2024 study found 620,000 jobs in 10 key sectors, including global business services, face significant change within three to five years. Aisyah is a sample persona; her questions are real.

  1. Which of my skills are still worth something?

    AI takes over tasks, not whole jobs. HalaTuju splits each skill into tasks and shows which ones AI already does, which it assists with, and which still need you.

  2. Where can I realistically go from here?

    Keyword matching misses real transfers. HalaTuju finds skill bridges, such as reconciliation leading into carbon accounting, because both trace every number back to a source document.

  3. Will this advice still be right next month?

    Career tests are one-off snapshots. HalaTuju's market scout checks for new AI capabilities daily, and your route updates when a change is approved.

How well you match a job

Match = Σ (skill weight × your coverage) ÷ Σ skill weight

Skill weights come from how often employers ask for a skill. Coverage is your level divided by the level the job needs.

  1. The market scout finds a signal

    It searches arXiv, OpenAlex and AI vendor news for each task it tracks, and must copy an exact quote and a source link.

  2. Fixed rules filter it

    No source, a quote that isn't on the page, or a duplicate: dropped. Small changes backed by two or more sources apply on their own.

  3. A second AI checks the claim

    A separate reviewer model compares the claim with its evidence quote and reports how confident it is.

  4. A person approves big changes

    Anything big, single-source, new, or that would change someone's route waits for a human reviewer.

  5. Everyone affected is rerouted

    Profiles are re-scored against the new knowledge version, and each user sees what changed and why.

Built for the Domain 1 brief

Aiversecity asked for a tool that tracks AI advancement, matches people to future roles, and builds reskilling pathways linked to the SDGs. Here is where each requirement lives in the app.

  • Skills profile intakeCV or LinkedIn upload, interviewer questions, level check
  • Dated AI-trend signalsMarket scout, review queue, versioned knowledge
  • Future-role matchingMatch % and skill gaps in weeks, per job
  • Personal reskilling pathsFastest, No-regret and Safest routes with courses
  • SDG mappingEvery job and skill tagged, with a one-line reason
  • More than one futureModerate vs fast AI, and a 2026–2030 slider

Guardrails we hold ourselves to

  • Suggest, don't decideNo path enters a learning plan until the worker or their advisor approves it.
  • No invented factsAgents can only pick skills, jobs and courses that exist in the database. A fact checker removes any claim without a source.
  • Fair by designHalaTuju never asks for or uses age, gender, race or religion.
  • Private by defaultPersonal details are removed from a CV before any AI model sees it. The demo uses synthetic data only.
