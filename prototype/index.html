Sample persona

Her company has announced AI invoice processing. She has 90 days to decide what to do and about six hours a week to learn.

  • Which of my skills still matter?HalaTuju splits each skill into tasks and shows which ones AI already does.
  • Where can I realistically go?It finds skill bridges into growing roles and ranks them by how well she matches.
  • Will the plan still be right next month?New AI signals, approved by a person, update her route with the source attached.

From CV to a personal route in minutes

Four steps, each one shown in the working prototype. Pick a step, or let the tour play.

Start from what you've already done

Upload a CV or LinkedIn PDF. The interviewer agent reads it and asks only about the gaps, then you confirm each skill on a five-level scale.

  • Personal details are removed before any AI model sees the file
  • Questions come only from skills the CV leaves unclear
  • Never asks for age, gender, race or religion

In the demo: first-time setup

See which roles fit, and why

Each match score comes from the skills employers ask for in that role, weighted by how often they appear in job postings.

  • What you already cover, and what is missing in weeks of learning
  • How much of the job AI does, assists with, or leaves to you
  • The SDGs each role supports, with a one-line reason

In the demo: click any job on the map

Choose how to get there

A shortest-path search over the skills network builds three routes to the same job, fitted to the hours you have each week.

  • Fastest, No-regret (holds up if AI moves fast) and Safest
  • Every step explains why your experience carries over
  • Courses with hours, cost and HRD Corp-claimable options

In the demo: the Path tab

Stay on course when the market moves

The market scout watches AI research and vendor news for each task it tracks. A person approves big changes, then every affected route is recalculated.

  • Every signal carries a dated source and an exact quote
  • Small, well-sourced changes apply on their own; big ones wait for review
  • Nothing enters your plan until you or your advisor approve it

Agents read, formulas score, people decide

AI does the reading and the talking. Every number on screen comes from plain code and a sourced record, so the same input always gives the same answer.

  1. Market scout

    Searches AI research and vendor news for each tracked task, and must copy an exact quote and link.

  2. Fixed rules

    Drop anything with no source, a quote that isn't on the page, or a duplicate.

  3. AI reviewer

    A second model checks the claim against its evidence and reports its confidence.

  4. Human approval

    Big, single-source or route-changing updates wait for a person.

  5. Reroute

    Profiles are re-scored on the new knowledge version, and each worker sees what changed.

How a match is scored

Match =Σ (skill weight × your coverage)Σ skill weight

Skill weight reflects how often employers ask for the skill. Coverage is your level divided by the level the job needs.

Guardrails

  • Suggest, don't decideNo route enters a learning plan until the worker or their advisor approves it.
  • No invented factsAgents can only choose skills, jobs and courses that exist in the database.
  • Fair by designHalaTuju never asks for or uses age, gender, race or religion.
  • Private by defaultPersonal details are removed from a CV before any AI model sees it.

Who HalaTuju serves

  • Primary

    Mid-career workers

    Starting with finance and admin roles exposed to AI, such as accounts payable, payroll and data entry.

  • Secondary

    Career advisors and HR teams

    Review and approve routes, and correct skill data with evidence.

  • Next

    Universities, TVET and reskilling programmes

    Guide students and cohorts using anonymised, aggregate views.
