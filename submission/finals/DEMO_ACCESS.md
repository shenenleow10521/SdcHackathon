## Try it in 2 minutes

1. Open the live app and choose **Upload my CV as a guest**.
2. Upload `priya-cv.pdf`. Confirm the skills.
3. **My skills**: see skills at risk, AI-assisted and still yours.
4. **Explore**: pick a job on the map, or search any of 3,039 jobs.
5. **Plan**: route, weeks and cost; switch Today / 2030 Slow / Steady / Fast; see the SDG contribution.
6. **My goal**: start a track and log a weekly check-in.

## If the live demo fails

1. Live URL on the phone hotspot.
2. Local build on the laptop: `bash demo/start.sh`, then open http://localhost:4330/app (own database, no scheduled researcher run).
3. Backup video, from the current step.

All data shown is synthetic or public. No real CVs are used.
