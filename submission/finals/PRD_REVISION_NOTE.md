## Revision 2: 31 October 2026 (final round)

| Area | Phase 1 (25 Oct) | Final (1 Nov) | Why we changed it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core question | "Where can I move?" | **Stay first, then move**: which of my skills AI takes, how to stay AI-ready, then where to go | Users and judges first ask "will AI take my job, and which part of it?" |
| Skill view | Matched skills and gaps | Every skill grouped as at risk, AI-assisted or still yours, with one action each | Gives a worker a next step for each skill, not only a score |
| Job coverage for routes | 16 sample jobs | Any of 3,039 ESCO occupations, with cross-field bridges reviewed before use | The sample catalogue could not answer "check my job" |
| Learning plan | 30 listed courses | Hand-checked learning links (all opened 30 Oct), practice tasks, AI-era skills with sources | The Domain 1 guide requires real free or low-cost resources |
| Map | Distance = similarity | Distance = months to job-ready (3/6/12/24-month rings) | Time matters more to a worker than similarity |
| Scenarios | Steady / Fast toggle, 1,000-future stress test | Today vs 2030 Slow / Steady / Fast side by side, with what shifts spelled out, and a futures chart | The guide asks for scenarios driven by parameters, with the shift shown |
| Evidence review | One AI reviewer plus fixed rules | Three voter agents on two vendors' models with an audit log; a person decides split votes | One reviewer accepted vendor marketing; a sceptic voter catches it |
| SDG mapping | Tags on 29 skills and 16 jobs | Every ESCO job and skill tagged with a reason; path contribution in Plan | The guide requires a reason for each tag and a path total |
| Progress | Saved plan | My goal: weekly check-ins, proof of each skill, opt-in HR team progress | Learner motivation and the HR view are good-to-haves in the rubric |
| Thin profiles | CV or pasted text | Plus a no-CV task quiz | Robustness for a thin profile |
| AI provider | Gemini | `[confirm]` KrackedDevs proxy (GPT + DeepSeek), Gemini as fallback | Free-tier quotas; a vote panel on two vendors' models |
